# Olist E-Commerce Analysis: Key Findings

Data: 99,441 orders, 96,096 customers, 3,095 sellers.
Tools: SQL (SQLite), Python, Pandas.

## 1. Late deliveries hurt customer reviews
- Late orders average 2.27 stars. On-time orders average 4.29.
- About 6.7% of delivered orders (6,410) arrive late.
- Recommendation: fix delivery delays first. They are the biggest driver of bad reviews.

## 2. Customers rarely come back
- Only 3.12% of customers (2,997 of 96,096) ordered more than once.
- Recommendation: add loyalty offers and follow-up emails.

## 3. Top categories
- health_beauty earns the most (about 1.26M).
- watches_gifts earns almost as much (1.21M) from far fewer items, so it has a high value per sale.
- Recommendation: promote high-value categories.

## 4. A few sellers drive most revenue
- The top 10 sellers earned between 135K and 229K each.
- Recommendation: support top sellers and learn from them.
